# Medi Bud AI Health Companion — Engineering Limitations & Boundary Disclosure

This document provides a transparent, academically rigorous assessment of the engineering boundaries, operational assumptions, and limitations of the Medi Bud prototype.

---

## 1. Educational Non-Clinical Scope

- **Not a Diagnostic Tool**: Medi Bud is an academic college engineering prototype built for lab report comprehension and wellness habit management. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment prescriptions, or clinical triage.
- **Mandatory Disclaimers**: Every response generated by the system includes an explicit disclaimer urging users to consult a certified physician for medical decisions.
- **Red-Flag Escalation**: In the presence of acute red-flag symptoms (e.g., crushing chest pain, sudden difficulty breathing, stroke indicators), the system terminates conversational generative output and immediately displays Indian emergency response hotlines (112, 108).

---

## 2. Report Parsing & Biomarker Extraction

- **Document Formats**: The extraction pipeline processes PDF documents, PNG, and JPEG images with a maximum size limit of 10 MB and 20 pages.
- **Machine-Printed vs. Handwritten**: The current normalizer is calibrated for standard machine-printed diagnostic panels (CBC, Lipid Panel, Blood Glucose, Metabolic Profile). Hand-written doctor notes or severely degraded scans require human review and manual value correction in the Observation Confirmation interface.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard**: Extracted biomarker values default to the `proposed` state and are never ingested into the retrieval index or chat grounding until explicitly verified and confirmed by the user.

---

## 3. Dietary & Nutritional Planning

- **Nutritional Provenance**: Nutritional estimates are calculated using reference values from the Indian Council of Medical Research - National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR-NIN) Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT).
- **Wellness Orientation**: The 7-day meal planner is designed for general adult wellness, variety, and declared allergen avoidance (peanuts, dairy, gluten, soy, mustard). It is not an intensive medical nutrition therapy (MNT) system for complex renal disease, severe diabetes, or post-surgical metabolic conditions.

---

## 4. Third-Party & Infrastructure Dependencies

- **OpenStreetMap Overpass API**: Geolocation discovery queries the public OSM Overpass API. To protect against public rate limiting, the backend employs a 15-minute in-memory cache and provides curated fallback facilities for major metropolitan areas.
- **LLM Provider Graceful Degradation**: If an external LLM key (`GEMINI_API_KEY`) is not configured, Medi Bud continues to function seamlessly in **Extractive Grounding Mode**, serving direct quoted excerpts from confirmed lab records and verified health guidelines without hallucination risk.
